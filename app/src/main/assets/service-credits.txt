Additional services

Road matching and backup street-map tiles: Geoapify (https://www.geoapify.com/).
Fallback road matching: Search by LocationIQ.com (https://locationiq.com).
LocationIQ road data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0 (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
LocationIQ Free permits commercial use with attribution and permanent storage of response data.
Request-response caching is limited to 48 hours on Free; this app caches it in memory for at most 60 seconds.
Map data: OpenStreetMap contributors (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
Backup Geoapify map tiles also use OpenMapTiles (https://openmaptiles.org/).
The backup map visibly credits Powered by Geoapify, OpenStreetMap contributors, and OpenMapTiles.
Geoapify use is limited to the configured free allowance. No automatic paid upgrade.

Backup country and first-level region display boundaries: geoBoundaries gbOpen,
William & Mary geoLab and contributors (https://www.geoboundaries.org/).
geoBoundaries is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Individual source licensing is checked against metadata before use. These backup
boundaries do not establish municipality visits or verified competitive credit.

Optional crash diagnostics: Firebase Crashlytics, Google.
https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
Crash reporting is off until enabled in Settings. Google Analytics is not included.

Optional friend notifications: Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google.
https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
Notifications are off until enabled in Settings. Weekly reminders run locally.
Provider availability switches: Cloudflare Workers KV.
Service uptime checks: UptimeRobot (no player data).
